The canonical glass panel: a dashed card with a three-column header.

**You provide:** `title`, the body as children, and optionally `meta`, `actions`, `footer` and `kind`.

- `title`: Chakra Petch uppercase.
- `meta`: a mono detail line in the centre column of the header. It truncates.
- `actions`: right-aligned header controls, such as icon buttons and badges.
- `kind`: a small mono tag before the title ("render", "queue").
- `footer` sits above a dashed rule.
- `glass` is the translucent fill with backdrop blur; `pad` is the padding in px, 10–14 in this system.
